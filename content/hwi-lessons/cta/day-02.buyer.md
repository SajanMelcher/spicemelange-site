## Apply this on the desk

Your walls are only as strong as their numbers. While training is still on paper, pick the one guard you feel least sure about and decide, on paper first, a value smaller than you think you need. Set it only when you're ready, then ask your bot to read every guard back to you. A wall you've chosen on purpose is far easier to trust at three in the morning.
