## Apply this on the desk

Write your first lesson today, even a small one, in the fixed format: id, applies, since, source, two sentences. Then put a date on your calendar a month from now to ask a single question of it: does this still hold? A notebook that gets reread is the desk's real memory.

Your copy of Leto's Secret Journals has a lessons file waiting. Copy today's lesson into it, so every seat on your desk reads the same memory.
