## Apply this on the desk

Make the drill a habit. Put a HALT practice on your calendar once a month, and add one line to your desk notes: the three situations in which you'll create the file without thinking twice. Knowing your stop in advance is what lets you rest when the market runs all night.

Run today's drill across all your seats: create `HALT` at the desk root as well as in your desk-kit folder, ask each seat to confirm it sees the file and will act on nothing new, then remove both yourself.
