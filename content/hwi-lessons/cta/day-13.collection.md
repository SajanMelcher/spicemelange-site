## Apply this on the desk

Before your next idea goes to the feed, read it once as a Truthsayer would: is every claim something you saw, and is the risk stated as plainly as the hope? Ask your bot to underline anything in your draft that it can't tie to a report. Credit follows honesty, never volume.
