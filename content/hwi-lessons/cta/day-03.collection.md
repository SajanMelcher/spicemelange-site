## Apply this on the desk

No loss sales only works if you're content to hold what you buy. Today, look at the pools in your `desk-kit.json` and ask yourself, for each one: would I be at peace holding this for a long time if it never came back? Keep only the ones where the answer is yes. That single habit turns R1 from a rule you tolerate into one you rely on.

Your Duncan Idaho seat can draft exact exit tickets for the lots your bot is holding today, each one written at or above cost plus fees, for you to read and check.

*Education only, not financial advice. Trading can lose money, including all of it.*
