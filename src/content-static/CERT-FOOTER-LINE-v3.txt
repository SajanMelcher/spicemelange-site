The Spice Melange trading desk trades on Sui with no leverage and no borrowed money. Certificate money is received in the issuer's own exchange account and then held: 38% as a USDC reserve on Sui and 62% in STRC on a third-party platform, and the desk's automated trading only rebalances back to that split. Certificate withdrawals are paid from the reserve and by selling STRC or other assets, even at a loss; the desk does not borrow to pay them.
