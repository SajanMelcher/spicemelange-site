## What you buy
- You pay **100 USDC on Sui** for one certificate. One payment, one certificate.
- The certificate is an NFT minted to your wallet as soon as your payment is confirmed. It has a serial number, "No. {serial}".
- It is our unsecured promise to pay you. You don't own any of our assets or accounts, and you get no vote or share of profits.
- **No cap.** We keep issuing certificates, with no set limit.
- **Perpetual.** There is no end date. A certificate keeps being credited sats until you redeem it.
- **Redeem any time**, whole certificates only. No partial withdrawals.

## Who can buy
- **Only the current Secret Journals license token counts.** It is our current, non-transferable license token. Current collection tokens first sold as The Dune Saga Collection are the same token and count.

## How to buy
- Buy through our contract, from the Sui wallet that holds your license. Send exactly **100 USDC on Sui**.
- Your license is **checked on-chain** when you buy through the contract. If it doesn't qualify, no certificate is minted.
- **Paying the contract directly** mints your certificate in the same transaction. Your license is checked on-chain in that same step.
- Payments sent the wrong way, such as the wrong amount, a payment from an exchange, or a payment from a wallet without a qualifying license, get no certificate. We refund them by hand, which can take time. We pay the network fee on refunds.
- If you send exactly 100 USDC to our payment address from a wallet holding a qualifying license instead of using the contract, our system checks the payment and mints your certificate automatically, usually within minutes; daily limits can apply.
- We can pause new sales or stop the automated minting key at any time. Pausing never blocks a withdrawal request.

## How your money is used
- **62%** buys STRC, a preferred stock, held on a third-party crypto platform. Its price and dividend can fall. Its daily dividends are added to the USDC reserve.
- **38%** is held as a USDC reserve on Sui. (Your payment first arrives in the issuer's own exchange account and is then moved to Sui.) It pays withdrawals. 38% stays in USDC on Sui and is only traded to rebalance back to 62/38. Agents automatically rebalance the USDC on Sui; STRC moves are made by hand. No leverage.
- **Certificate money is never used to repay debt.**
- **Where it is held:** certificate money is held in the issuer's own exchange and wallet accounts. It is not segregated, not in a trust and not in a separate account. Nothing is set aside in your name.
- **If the reserve runs short**, we cover withdrawals in this order: the cash reserve, then selling STRC, then selling other assets, possibly at a loss. We never borrow to pay withdrawals.
- The Spice Melange trading desk trades on Sui with no leverage and no borrowed money. Agents automatically rebalance the USDC on Sui; STRC moves are made by hand.

## How the 8% is credited
- We aim to credit **8% a year** on 100 USDC. Simple interest, actual days out of 365, starting the day after you buy.
- Each day's amount (about $0.0219) is turned into **whole bitcoin sats, rounded down**.
- We use a public market price for bitcoin taken near **4:00 PM Pacific** each day, within 60 minutes of it. Each day's price is recorded once and never changed. If no price qualifies, that day is credited later at its own price.
- Rounding down means you get **a bit less than 8%**, about 7.3% to 7.9%. The higher bitcoin goes, the more rounding takes.
- **Every week** we move bitcoin equal to that week's credited sats into a separate holding used only for withdrawals. Up to a week of sats may not be set aside yet.
- That holding is still **ours**. It is not held in your name, not legally separate and not insured. Our creditors could reach it.
- **Our private ledger is the official record** of your sats. Numbers on-chain or on a web page may lag.
- Sats keep being credited while your withdrawal is reviewed, and stop once your payout is approved.
- The 8% is a target, not a promise. We may not be able to pay it.

## How to withdraw
1. **Request on-chain.** From the Sui wallet that holds your certificate, send a redemption request through our contract. This is a blockchain transaction and costs a small Sui gas fee, which you pay. The contract holds your certificate while the request is open.
2. **Sign the request.** On our holder page, sign the request message with the same wallet. It names your serial, payout asset, payout address and network.
3. **Confirm by email.** Confirm the request by email from the address on file (the email you used to buy), to **reserve@thespicemelange.org**. Give your serial number, pick **USDC on Sui** or **BTC**, and give your payout address and network. We ask you to confirm the address and network **twice**. A changed address needs a new signed request and two new confirmations.
4. **Stay safe.** **We will never ask for your recovery phrase or private key.**
5. **Approval.** Sajan Melcher approves each payout by hand and sends it through the exchange we use for payouts. Nothing is paid automatically.
6. **Timing.** We aim to pay **within 6 hours** of a verified request. That is a target, not a promise. It can take longer if assets must be sold or if we can't pay.

**What you get**
- **USDC (on Sui only):** 100 USDC, plus your sats turned into USDC, rounded down to the cent.
- **BTC:** 100 USDC turned into bitcoin, plus your sats.
- Bitcoin is priced at **the payout exchange's market price when the payout is made**. The amount we approve is an estimate. If bitcoin moves before we pay, the final amount moves too. (The daily 4 PM price is only for crediting sats.)
- **We pay the network fee on payouts.**
- We send only to the address you confirmed. If that address is wrong, we can't get it back.
- After payment, the certificate held by the contract is burned or marked redeemed, and earns nothing more.

**If we can't pay everyone in full**, requests are paid in the order they were verified. A request may be paid in part, with the rest paid when we have the funds.

## Risks
- **You can lose money.** Some or all of your 100 USDC.
- **One small issuer.** Payment depends on our finances. No insurance, no guarantor, no collateral pledged to you. The reserve and the bitcoin holding are not legally walled off.
- **Not on demand.** Every payout needs an on-chain request, a signed request, email confirmation and a manual approval. Payouts can be late.
- **Open requests.** While your request is open, the contract holds your certificate and only we can complete or reject it. You can't cancel it yourself.
- **STRC.** Its price and dividend can fall or stop. Its issuer carries heavy bitcoin and debt exposure. A loss on STRC means less to pay you.
- **Bitcoin.** It swings hard. Your sats and any BTC payout can lose value fast. If you choose BTC, you carry the bitcoin price risk, and what you get can be worth less than $100.
- **Trading.** The Spice Melange trading desk trades on Sui with no leverage and no borrowed money. Its automated trading only rebalances certificate funds back to the 62/38 split, and rebalancing trades can lose money. Certificate money is never used to repay debt.
- **Exchanges and custodians.** Certificate money sits in our own accounts at third-party exchanges and custodians. They can freeze, lose or be hacked out of funds, or change their rules or go offline. That can delay or stop payouts, or cause losses.
- **Other creditors.** You are unsecured. We have other debts and creditors, who may be paid before you, and there may be less or nothing left for holders.
- **Rounding.** Daily rounding keeps your rate a bit under 8%.
- **No resale.** There is no market for certificates and you can't transfer them.
- **Run risk.** With no cap and redemption any time, the total we owe can grow past our reserve. If many holders ask at once, for example after a bitcoin or STRC drop or bad news about us, the reserve can run out. We may have to sell STRC or other assets at a loss, miss the 6-hour aim by days or more, and pay later requests late or only in part.
- **No end date.** We never have to repay you on a set date. You get paid only by asking to redeem.
- **Tech.** Bugs, network outages, lost wallets or stolen keys can cause loss. If you lose the wallet that holds your certificate, you may not be able to request a withdrawal.
- **Minting key and contract.** Certificates are minted by an automated key through our smart contract. A bug or a stolen key could stop minting or redemptions, or create invalid certificates. We will not honour certificates that were not validly bought from us.
- **USDC.** It can lose its dollar peg or be frozen by its issuer.
- **One person.** One person runs the desk with AI agents and approves every payout. Mistakes, outages or his absence can delay or stop payment.
- **Regulators.** A regulator could decide this needs registration or a license. That could force us to stop, change or unwind it, maybe at a loss to you.
- **Taxes.** Sats and BTC payouts may be taxable, including on price changes.
- **No advice.** Nothing here is investment, tax or legal advice.
