# Day 13 · Credit, and what it isn't

In *Dune*, standing among the Fremen is earned by deeds, not bought. Desk credit works in a similar spirit. It's recognition for useful contributions to the feed.

## How credit is earned
- Ideas don't earn points automatically. There's no algorithm counting likes or clicks.
- Points are **granted** by the desk when an idea was genuinely useful: clear, well-reasoned, honest about risk, and helpful to other members.
- Grants are recorded, and the contributors list on the site shows who has earned what, under pseudonyms.

## What credit is
- A thank-you and a reputation signal: it shows that your ideas have been useful.
- A way to find thoughtful contributors in the feed.

## What credit is **not**
Please read this part twice:
- **Not cashable.** Points can't be withdrawn, sold, traded or exchanged for money, coins or tokens.
- **Not an investment.** Points don't earn interest, don't represent a share of anything and don't promise any future value.
- **Not a measure of trading profit.** A well-reasoned `watch` idea can earn credit, and a lucky guess may not.
- **Not a reason to post more.** The daily cap exists so quality wins over volume.

If anyone ever offers to buy your points, or sell you some, that's not from the desk. Ignore it, and feel free to report it.

## Why have credit at all?
Because a shared feed is only as good as its best contributors, and it's nice to say thank you. The desk keeps it simple on purpose: a human decides, points are visible, and nothing about them touches money.

## Your contribution counts even without points
Reading carefully, reporting a misleading idea, and writing honest lessons all make the desk better. Most of the value you'll get from these two weeks is in your own understanding, not in any score.

## Remember
- Credit is granted by the desk, not earned automatically.
- Points are non-cashable and not an investment.
- Quality over volume, always.

*Education only, not financial advice. Credit points have no cash value. No returns are promised or implied.*
