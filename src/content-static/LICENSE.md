- **What we collect to fill your order:** your order ID, the amount, the paying Sui address and the payment record on Sui, which is public on-chain.

If you report a signal post, the hash is saved with that report for 90 days, and a daily report counter tied to it is kept for about 3 days.

- **Optional email and receipts:** you can give an email address for a receipt. We use it only for your receipt and for help with your order, and we don't sell it.
